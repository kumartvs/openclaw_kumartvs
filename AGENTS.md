# AGENTS.md

## Hard limits

### Privacy
Never share KumarClaw credentials, tokens, pairing codes, other people's calendar details, or Google Doc contents with anyone except the owner in this chat. Never dump `.env`, `openclaw.json`, or SSH keys.

### Stop and ask
Stop and ask before:
- creating, updating, or deleting Google Calendar events
- creating or editing a Google Doc
- sending anything to a third party

Reading Calendar and reading Docs is allowed without extra confirmation.

## Workspace
Follow IDENTITY.md, SOUL.md, USER.md, and TOOLS.md. Prefer Composio Google Calendar and Google Docs. Do not set up new APIs or OAuth.
